Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Introduction

Toyo Specialty Store ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our store website and place orders, in compliance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

2. Information We Collect

When you process an order through our checkout system, we collect personal details necessary to fulfill your transaction and deliver your items. This includes:

  • Full Name
  • Delivery Address
  • Contact Phone Number
  • Proof of payment screenshots/receipts uploaded during checkout

3. How We Use Your Information

We process your information strictly for legitimate e-commerce purposes, including:

  • Processing, verifying, and fulfilling your merchandise orders.
  • Validating payment transactions using your uploaded e-wallet screenshots.
  • Managing customer records and tracking order history.
  • Logistics, shipping, and delivery arrangements.
  • Resolving disputes or contacting you regarding your purchase status.

4. Data Retention and Deletion Policy

We retain your personal data and uploaded payment proofs only for as long as necessary to fulfill the transactions, complete delivery, and address any immediate post-purchase inquiries or accounting requirements.

Once your order has been successfully completed, verified, and delivered, all sensitive identifiers, contact details, and uploaded payment screenshots will be permanently deleted from our active database and storage systems within 30 days.

5. Data Storage and Security

Order submissions and transaction details are securely recorded and organized using integrated spreadsheet and database storage systems. We implement reasonable technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction during the retention window.

6. Your Rights as a Data Subject

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012, you have the right to be informed, to object to processing, to access your data, and to request early correction or complete deletion of your personal records held by us prior to the standard retention period.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or the handling and deletion of your data, please feel free to reach out to our store support team.
